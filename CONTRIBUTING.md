# Contributing

This repository adopts the
[Varinomics contribution policy](https://github.com/Varinomics/vnm_terminal_surface/blob/master/docs/contributions/contribution-policy.md).
Contributors keep ownership and grant the rights needed for GPL and
alternative licensing, including proprietary terms and downstream
sublicensing. The repository's [GPL licence](LICENSE) remains in effect
for its GPL distribution.

Each human author and co-author needs the verified
[individual CLA v1.0](https://github.com/Varinomics/vnm_terminal_surface/blob/master/docs/contributions/individual-cla-v1.0.md).
Employer/client rights also require the appropriate
[corporate CLA and designated-contributor authorisation](https://github.com/Varinomics/vnm_terminal_surface/blob/master/docs/contributions/corporate-cla-v1.0.md).
Send the complete executed form privately to **info@varinomics.com** as it
instructs; see the
[contribution privacy notice](https://github.com/Varinomics/vnm_terminal_surface/blob/master/docs/contributions/privacy.md).

Identify all authors and disclose imported or adapted material in the
pull-request template. Maintainers review its actual licence separately from
CLA coverage. Record origins and obligations in this repository's
[THIRD_PARTY/](THIRD_PARTY/) and
[third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), following the
[import procedure](https://github.com/Varinomics/vnm_terminal_surface/blob/master/docs/contributions/third-party-materials.md).

Required statuses are `cla/coverage (records N)` and
`contributions/material-review (records N)`, using the registry generation
`N` required by the repository's merge rules.
[Agreement administration](https://github.com/Varinomics/vnm_terminal_surface/blob/master/docs/contributions/administration.md)
describes private execution evidence, versioned coverage, corporate rosters
and exact-subject exceptions. This policy does not establish missing rights
or signatures for earlier commits.
